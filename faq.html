Frequently Asked Questions

What does The God's Tool do?

It cleans messy data. You upload a CSV or connect a Google Sheet or database, it lists the problems it finds, and you approve the fixes or describe your own in plain English. The steps can be saved and run again.

Can I clean a CSV or Google Sheet for free?

Yes. The free plan handles CSV files up to 5 MB and 5,000 rows per run, with up to 3 sources and 1 pipeline. New accounts also get 14 days with everything unlocked.

Do I need to know SQL or code?

No. You can do all of it by clicking and typing in plain English. If you do know SQL, every step shows the query behind it and you can edit it.

What are credits, and who are they for?

One credit covers one AI action, like asking it to write a cleaning step. They're useful if you only clean data now and then, because there is no monthly fee. They cost about $0.03 each, with a $2 minimum top-up.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes, plenty of freelancers do. The files are processed under your account, so check that your agreement with the client allows sharing their data with a tool like this.

Is my data safe?

Data is encrypted in transit and we don't sell it or share it for marketing. Google sign-in is only used to log you in, and a Google Sheet is read only when you connect it yourself. The AI features run in private mode: they get your instruction, column names and a summary of each column's structure, never your actual rows. Read how it works in plain words, with diagrams, on our blog, or see the privacy policy for the details.

Does the AI see my data?

Not by default. The AI gets your instruction and a description of each column's structure, then writes cleaning steps that we run on our side. Your rows and cell values aren't sent to it. A workspace admin can switch on sharing a few sample rows for better results on value-based fixes, and it stays off until they do. The blog post "How AI cleaning works without ever seeing your data" explains the whole path with diagrams.

Which data sources can I connect?

CSV, Google Sheets, MySQL, PostgreSQL, BigQuery, MongoDB, AWS RDS, S3, DynamoDB and Redshift, Google Cloud Storage, Cloud SQL, Databricks, REST APIs and Google Analytics 4.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your account moves to the free plan with its limits. Nothing is deleted, and you can upgrade or buy credits whenever you like.

How does a quote work?

Fill in the short form on this page. We read it and reply by email with a price for credits, seats or sources. There's no obligation to buy.
