Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Welcome to The God's Tool ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform at thegodstool.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use our service:

  • Email address and name (when you register or sign in with Google)
  • Data you upload or connect (CSV files, Google Sheets, database connections)
  • Usage data such as features used and AI transform counts
  • Payment information processed securely through Razorpay

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our data cleaning and analysis services
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To send you transactional emails (password resets, billing)
  • To enforce usage limits based on your plan

3. Google Data

If you connect Google Sheets, we access only the sheets you explicitly select. We do not store your Google credentials. Access tokens are used solely to read and process data you request and are never shared with third parties.

4. Data Storage

Your account data is stored securely in MongoDB Atlas. Uploaded files are processed in memory and not permanently stored on our servers unless explicitly saved by you.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Razorpay – payment processing
  • Resend – transactional email delivery
  • Google OAuth – sign-in authentication
  • Railway – cloud hosting infrastructure

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

8. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at vatsaman888@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact
