Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Welcome to The God's Tool ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform at thegodstool.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use our service:

  • Email address and name (when you register or sign in with Google)
  • Data you upload or connect (CSV files, Google Sheets, database connections)
  • Usage data such as features used and AI transform counts
  • Payment information processed securely through Razorpay

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our data cleaning and analysis services
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To send you transactional emails (password resets, billing)
  • To enforce usage limits based on your plan

3. Google Data

If you connect Google Sheets, we access only the sheets you explicitly select via Google's file picker. We do not store your Google account credentials (password). Access tokens are used solely to read and process the spreadsheet data you request, and are never shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising purposes.

Sheet data may be temporarily processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to perform the specific cleaning, transformation, or analysis feature you request. This data is not used to train, develop, or improve any AI/ML models, and is not retained by our AI provider beyond what is required to complete your request.

3.1 Limited Use Compliance

The God's Tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage

Your account data is stored securely in MongoDB Atlas. Uploaded files and imported Google Sheets data are processed in memory and are not permanently stored on our servers unless explicitly saved by you. Imported spreadsheet data is retained only for the duration of your active session/processing and is not kept longer than necessary to provide the requested feature.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Razorpay – payment processing
  • Resend – transactional email delivery
  • Google OAuth – sign-in authentication
  • Anthropic – AI-powered data cleaning and transformation
  • Railway – cloud hosting infrastructure

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data — including Google user data — with any third parties for marketing, advertising, or lending purposes. Data may be processed by our infrastructure and AI providers listed above, solely to deliver app functionality, under terms that prohibit further use or sharing.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

8. Your Rights

You may disconnect your Google account at any time from within the app, and may request deletion of your account and associated data (including any connected Google data) at any time by contacting us at vatsaman888@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at vatsaman888@gmail.com
