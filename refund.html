Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [Insert Date]

At The God's Tool, we want you to be satisfied with our platform. This policy outlines the terms under which refunds and cancellations are handled for subscriptions purchased directly on thegodstool.com.

1. Direct Subscription Purchases

If you purchase a subscription plan (Lite, Starter, Pro, Team, or Enterprise) directly through thegodstool.com, you are eligible for a full refund if requested within 14 days of your initial purchase date.

To be eligible:

  • The refund request must be made within 14 days of the original purchase
  • This applies to your first purchase of a plan; renewals are non-refundable unless the service was unavailable due to an error on our end
  • Refunds are not available for any usage-based add-ons or overage charges already incurred

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at [support email] with:

  • The email address associated with your account
  • Your order/transaction ID
  • A brief reason for the refund request (helps us improve)

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 2 business days.

3. Refund Processing

Once approved, refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Razorpay. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take 5-10 business days for the refund to reflect in your account.

4. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard under Settings → Billing. Cancelling stops future billing but:

  • Does not automatically trigger a refund for the current billing cycle
  • Your plan will remain active until the end of the current billing period
  • If cancellation happens within the 14-day refund window, you may still request a refund per Section 1

5. Lifetime Deal (LTD) Purchases

For Lifetime Deal purchases made through AppSumo or other marketplace partners, refunds are governed by that marketplace's own refund policy (e.g., AppSumo's standard refund window), not this policy. Please refer to the marketplace's terms for LTD-specific refund eligibility.

6. Exceptions

We may deny a refund request if we detect:

  • Abuse of the refund policy (e.g., repeated purchase-and-refund cycles)
  • Violation of our Terms of Service
  • Fraudulent payment activity
