Why a plain converter is not enough

A PDF does not store a table. It stores letters at positions on a page, and a converter has to guess which letters belong in which cell. When it guesses wrong the result still looks like a clean spreadsheet: a debit lands in the credit column, a long description swallows the next row, a row goes missing at a page break. Nothing tells you, and you find out when the totals don't match, if you ever look.

A bank statement can check itself, though. Every row prints a running balance, so the previous balance plus this row's amount has to equal this row's balance. If a number was misread, that sum breaks on that row.

How it works

  1. Drop in the PDF. It is read on our server in a few seconds. No AI service is involved and the file is not stored.
  2. Look at the marks. A tick means the row is proven by the balances. An exclamation mark means it needs a look. Click it and the PDF page opens beside the table with the row outlined, plus a note on what might have gone wrong.
  3. Fix and keep. Correct a number in the table and the check runs again. Then download a CSV that opens in Excel, or use it in the app to clean it further.

What gets checked

  • The balance after each row against the previous balance and that row's amount.
  • The opening balance, the closing balance and any printed totals against the sum of the rows.
  • A second, independent reading of every line, with a flag where the two disagree.
  • Dates. If 03/04/2024 could be 3 April or 4 March and the other dates don't settle it, you are asked instead of it guessing.
  • Likely causes for a break: debit and credit swapped, two digits swapped, a row lost at a page break, a duplicate row.

What it cannot do

  • It cannot catch a mistake that still adds up. That is why the PDF stays next to the table.
  • Scanned or photographed statements are refused. They have no text to read, and guessing digits from an image is how wrong numbers get through.
  • Statements with no running balance, such as most credit card statements, are not supported yet. Without balances there is nothing to prove a row against.
  • It does not import into accounting software. You get a CSV, which you can then clean or load wherever you like.

Questions

How do I convert a bank statement PDF to Excel without errors?

No converter can promise zero errors, so the useful thing is to check the result. A bank statement prints a balance after every row, so the previous balance plus this row's amount must equal this row's balance. The tool runs that check on every row and flags the ones that fail, with a likely cause.

Does it use AI to read my statement?

No. The PDF's own text is read on our server without an AI service, and the file is not stored. Only a table you choose to save is kept, as a normal CSV source in your workspace.

Can it read scanned or photographed statements?

Not yet. A scan has no text to read, so it is refused instead of guessed. Download the PDF from your bank's website or app.

What does "proven" mean?

A row is proven when the previous balance plus its amount equals its printed balance. It does not mean the row is perfect. A mistake that still adds up would pass, which is why the PDF page is shown next to the table.

Which banks does it work with?

It reads the layout of the table rather than a list of banks. The statement needs a header row with a date column, a balance column, and either debit and credit columns or one amount column. If a statement doesn't read correctly, send it to contact@thegodstool.com and we will look at it.
