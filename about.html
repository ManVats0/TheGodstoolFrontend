About The God's Tool

The God's Tool is data cleaning software built by Aman Sharma, a data analyst in India. He built it because the cleaning in his own job did not stop at one kind of file: it was databases, Excel and PDFs, all at once.

Updated 13 November 2026.

Why it exists

In his own work, Aman Sharma kept meeting the same problem in three different shapes. Some data sat in databases, some in Excel files, and some arrived as PDFs. All of it needed cleaning before anyone could trust a report, and most tools handle only one of the three.

So he started building one tool that covers all of them: connect a database, upload a CSV or a Google Sheet, or drop in a PDF with a table, then find what is wrong and fix it in plain English.

Who builds it

Aman Sharma is a data analyst at a government organisation in India. He has a BCA from Kurukshetra University and has taken data science courses from international universities, which showed him how much of analysis is cleaning. He built The God's Tool on his own. It is his project and is not an official product of his employer or endorsed by it.

How it was tested

The tool was tested by freelance software testers, and their feedback shaped what you see now. The PDF table reader has so far been tried on generated files and on one real bank statement format, so check its output against your PDF. We would rather say that than claim every bank and every layout works.

What we do not promise

We do not say that any hosted service, ours included, is safe whatever you do with it. If your data is very sensitive, do these before you upload:

  • Remove or mask personal columns you do not need for the cleaning.
  • Use the cleaning steps that do not use AI, or keep AI in private mode.
  • Avoid typing real names or numbers into the instruction box and avoid naming columns after sensitive details, because both are sent as written.
  • Check that your agreement with a client or your employer allows you to share the data with a tool like this.

What it is not

It is software you operate yourself, not a done-for-you cleaning service. It does not read scanned pages or photos, and it is not a data warehouse or an ETL platform for moving large volumes of data.
